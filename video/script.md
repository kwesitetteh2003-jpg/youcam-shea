## What is live

All app footage is a real screen recording of https://youcam-glow.vercel.app: an actual selfie upload, a live YouCam Skin Analysis + Skin Tone Analysis call, the generated plan, and a live YouCam Clothes VTO render. Nothing is mocked. The only speed change is the try-on wait, which is sped up 4x and labelled on screen.

The try-on uses the app's sample full-length model photo (Marlon Schmeiski / Pexels), not Ama.
